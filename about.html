About ICE Campus Events

A simple platform for discovering and managing campus events.

ICE

ICE Campus Events

ICE Campus Events is a campus event discovery platform designed to help students easily find upcoming events, workshops, seminars, contests and cultural programs.

Students can explore events by category, view event details, save interesting events and add events to their personal schedule.

Discover events based on different categories.

Save Events

Save events that you want to attend later.

T

My Schedule

Add events to your schedule and keep track of them.

Our Purpose

Our goal is to make campus event discovery easier, organized and accessible for students. Instead of searching through different notices and announcements, students can find important campus events from one platform.
